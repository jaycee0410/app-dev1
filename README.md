
# 🐉 How to Train Your Dragon 2 (2014)

**Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Fantasy

**Director:** Dean DeBlois

## Description

*How to Train Your Dragon 2* follows Hiccup and his dragon, Toothless, as they discover a hidden world of dragons. Their adventure takes an unexpected turn when they encounter a dangerous enemy who threatens their peaceful home.

> A story about friendship, courage, family, and adventure.

**My Rating:** ⭐ 9/10
